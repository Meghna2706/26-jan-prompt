# Requirements Document: Mandi AI Assistant Platform

## Introduction

The Mandi AI Assistant Platform is a production-ready web application designed to empower farmers and traders in India with AI-driven market intelligence, price discovery, and negotiation assistance. The platform provides multilingual support, real-time market data, automated alerts, and intelligent recommendations to help users make informed trading decisions.

## Glossary

- **System**: The Mandi AI Assistant Platform web application
- **User**: Any person accessing the platform (Farmer, Trader, or Admin)
- **Farmer**: User role focused on selling agricultural products
- **Trader**: User role focused on buying/selling agricultural products
- **Admin**: User role with platform management capabilities
