# Requirements Document: Enterprise Mandi AI Platform

## Introduction

The Enterprise Mandi AI Platform is a comprehensive digital marketplace solution designed to empower farmers, traders, and administrators in India's agricultural sector. The platform leverages AI-driven insights, real-time market data, and multilingual support to facilitate transparent price discovery, intelligent negotiation, and data-driven decision-making. The system serves three distinct user roles with tailored dashboards, features, and workflows while maintaining enterprise-grade security, scalability, and accessibility.

## Glossary

- **User**: Any person interacting with the platform (Farmer, Trader, or Admin)
- **Farmer**: User role focused on selling agricultural products and discovering market prices
- **Trader**: User role focused on buying agricultural products and market analysis
- **Admin**: User role with system management and oversight capabilities
- **JWT**: JSON Web Token used for stateless authentication
- **RBAC**: Role-Based Access Control system for authorization
- **Price_Discovery**: Process of finding current market prices for agricultural commodities
- **Negotiation_Assistant**: AI-powered feature providing negotiation suggestions
- **Market_Trends**: Historical and predictive market data analysis
- **WebSocket**: Bidirectional communication protocol for real-time updates
- **Multilingual_Support**: System capability to display content in multiple languages
- **Dashboard**: Personalized landing page after us
